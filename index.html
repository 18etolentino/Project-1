About Me

Hi! I'm Evelin Tolentino, an incoming MIMS student at UC Berkeley and Human Systems Engineering graduate from Arizona State University (Magna Cum Laude, 3.6 GPA). I design at the intersection of research, systems thinking, and human experience, specializing in UX research, human-centered design, and mixed-methods research for complex systems and underserved populations.

My current work centers on Listo, an AI-assisted legal readiness tool for undocumented immigrants, rooted in primary research conducted in Spanish with community-based participants, combining mixed-methods study design and prototype testing. My previous projects apply behavioral economics to circular design systems, real client UX audits, and functional AI products.

I spent 4+ years as a professional CAD designer and project coordinator in residential architecture, producing construction-ready documentation for luxury residential interiors across concurrent high-stakes projects. That precision is in everything I build.

Architecture taught me that every structure has a logic. UX taught me that every user has a story. I build at the place where both are true.

My UX Design Process

  1. Empathize – Conduct user research, interviews, and observations
  2. Define – Analyze insights to identify user needs and problems
  3. Ideate – Sketch, brainstorm, and explore design possibilities
  4. Prototype – Build wireframes and interactive mockups
  5. Test – Conduct usability testing and iterate based on feedback
